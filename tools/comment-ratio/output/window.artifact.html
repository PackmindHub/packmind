Daily figures are noisy: a day is one or two merged pull requests, and a single documentation-heavy file moves it several points. Read the level over a week, not the last dot. The two figures to watch are the median day (16.8% before the change) and the pooled ratio over all the days (15.7% before), which weights each day by how much it wrote.

18 September is off the chart and out of both figures: a deliberate pass over existing comments, not a day of ordinary authoring. It reached 47.3% and is in the table above, tagged as such — one day at that height sets the axis and flattens the few points of movement this page exists to show.
